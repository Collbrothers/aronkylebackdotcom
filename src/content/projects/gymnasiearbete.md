My final-year thesis (gymnasiearbete) investigates how infill density and
print speed affect the tensile strength of FDM-printed PLA parts, tested
against ISO 527.

Thesis is currently undergoing, this page will be updated alongside the work. The paper will be available June 2027.

## Methodology

Testing will adhere to ISO 527-1 and take place at [Research Institutes of Sweden](https://www.ri.se/en) (RISE). Specimen conform to ISO 527-2 Type 1A, likewise there will be 5 duplicates of each specific specimen in accordance with ISO 527-1.

The chosen variables and their values are:

| Speed | Infill |
|-------|--------|
| 80    | 20     |
| 200   | 60     |
| 300   | 100    |

That results in 9 combinations across 5 specimen of each combination, resulting in a total of 45 specimen.

Specimen will be printed on a Prusa Core One+, with Prusament Galaxy Black PLA, with preprocessing consisting of a visual inspection of filament roll and drying for 6 hours at 53 &#8451; with a maximum rH% of 20%.
